🎤 lyricsdisplay - Real-time synchronized lyrics for music

Download for Windows

lyricsdisplay shows your song lyrics on your screen while you listen to music. It supports many music players and services. The lyrics move in sync with the audio. You get an overlay that stays on top of other windows. This makes it a tool for karaoke or reading along.

✨ Key Features

  • Sync: The application follows the music timing.
  • Karaoke Mode: Words highlight as the singer sings them.
  • Overlay: The window stays above your other programs.
  • Compatibility: It works with Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music.
  • Design: The interface stays minimal and clean.

📥 Installation Guide

Follow these steps to set up the software on your Windows computer:

  1. Visit the official releases page to view the latest versions.
  2. Look for the file ending in .exe under the Assets section.
  3. Click the file name to download it to your computer.
  4. Open your Downloads folder and double-click the file to start the setup.
  5. Follow the prompts on your screen to complete the installation.
  6. Launch the program from your desktop shortcut or the Start menu.

💻 System Requirements

Your computer must meet these basic standards to run the application:

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • Memory: At least 4GB of RAM.
  • Processor: A modern dual-core chip or faster.
  • Internet: A connection to fetch lyrics data.

🛠 Usage Tips

Once you run the app, pick your music source from the menu. The app detects active audio players. If it does not show lyrics, confirm your music player is open. You can drag the overlay window to any part of your screen. Use the settings menu to change the font size or the text color. The app saves your layout choices for the next time you open it.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app stop working if I close my music player? No, but it waits until you start a new track to show updated lyrics.

Can I change the overlay transparency? Yes, look for the 'Appearance' tab in the settings menu.

Does the app require a login? No, the app operates without a user account.
